What people ask

Do I have to learn all the gestures?
Start with one: hold your mouse’s scroll-wheel button and move up or down to change speed. Try the others when you need them.
Will it work with my setup?
Use illi on YouTube in Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium browser on your desktop or laptop. Use a mouse with a scroll-wheel button, choose right-click activation in settings, or use your trackpad.
